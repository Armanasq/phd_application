## 1. Purpose and scope

The README describes a personal archive associated with a doctoral program in Information Science. It organizes application materials, interview preparation, coursework, general-exam preparation, research planning, and year-by-year doctoral progress.

This is not an admissions automation system or a general university policy guide. Its value is as a single-case reference for how an individual organized application artifacts and post-admission academic work.

## 2. Relevant material inspected

- README and repository structure.
- Interview notes, which are a preparation question bank rather than a verified transcript of questions actually asked.
- Year 1 and Year 2 planning notes.
- General-exam README describing a post-admission milestone and prospectus preparation.
- Acknowledgements file was identified but personal names were not reproduced.

The application-materials directory contains personal CV and statement documents. Those PDFs were not opened, quoted, or copied. The repository also contains personal academic materials that should not be mirrored into the public PhD Atlas corpus.

## 3. Reusable observations

- The archive separates application materials from interview preparation, coursework, research notes, and post-admission milestones.
- Interview preparation is represented as a question bank; it can inform a preparation checklist, but it does not prove that those questions were asked or that a particular answer led to admission.
- Year-by-year planning makes post-admission workload and milestone tracking explicit.
- The general-exam notes distinguish a doctoral program milestone from the admissions process itself.

These are structural observations, not verified admissions success factors. No denominator, rejected applications, program-cycle metadata, reviewer feedback, or causal outcome evidence is supplied in the inspected materials.

## 4. Privacy, provenance, and limitations

The repository contains applicant-specific documents and acknowledgements. Public accessibility does not establish permission to redistribute personal statements, CVs, recommendation letters, or identifying information. No license or reuse grant was identified. Do not mirror the personal documents.

The review did not inspect the PDF contents, verify the author's admission outcome, validate current program policy, or interview the author. The repository is a single-person archive and cannot support population-level conclusions.

## 5. Disposition

Retain as a preliminary firsthand resource for interview-preparation structure and post-admission planning. Do not classify it as an independently verified admissions guide or reuse its personal documents. Upgrade only if the relevant public process material can be inspected without reproducing private/copyrighted content and the source provenance is established.
