## Purpose and contents

A single-README curated list of data-science scholarships for master's degrees, PhDs and courses. The list links to university scholarships, Google/Microsoft/IBM/Meta doctoral fellowships, data-science funding aggregators, study programs and fellowship resources.

The latest commit is 2 February 2021. No code, test suite, workflow or license file was established.

## Research value

The list is useful as a seed source for fellowship names and funding-program discovery, especially technology-company doctoral fellowships and data-science-specific awards. It also demonstrates that discipline-specific funding should be searched separately from general PhD vacancies.

## Limitations

Many linked pages are dated 2019–2021 or point to broad scholarship portals rather than a specific current cycle. There are no source snapshots, publication dates, checked timestamps, award-year fields, applicant-class eligibility, funding amounts or status states. A link's presence does not establish that the award is open, funded in the current cycle or available to an international applicant.

## PhD Atlas decision

Keep only as a historical discovery index. Revalidate each named award against its official current-cycle page before creating a record. Store official funder, cycle, eligible applicant class, award components, deadline, evidence excerpt and last-checked timestamp. Do not use aggregator summaries as eligibility evidence.
