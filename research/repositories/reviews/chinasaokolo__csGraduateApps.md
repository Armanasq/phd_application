## Purpose and contents

A curated list of applicant-support programs for prospective US computer-science graduate students, especially PhD applicants with limited access to external application support. It includes department and university programs, deadlines, degree scope, status-unknown labels, and resources on rankings, SOPs, CVs, recommendations, fellowships and preparation.

The README explicitly says it was still being updated for the 2024–2025 cycle. The latest commit is 10 November 2024. The repository declares CC BY-NC 4.0.

## Research value

This is not a general scholarship or vacancy directory. Its contribution is a distinct applicant-support layer: mentorship, application fee/support programs, writing guidance, fellowship discovery and access to graduate-application advice. It is useful for a PhD Atlas support-resource catalogue, particularly where a program serves applicants from underrepresented backgrounds.

The author's stated decision rule—identify multiple faculty of interest and prioritize research fit over rankings—is a useful personal perspective, not an institutional admissions rule.

## Limitations

The table contains deadlines from October–November 2024 and many entries marked TBD or Status Unknown. Those values cannot be carried forward to 2026–2027. Some links point to general admissions pages or forms rather than a current program announcement. There is no structured cycle history, source snapshot, eligibility evidence or automated update process.

## PhD Atlas decision

Create a separate `ApplicantSupportProgram` type rather than mixing these resources with funded PhD vacancies. Store applicant population, support type, cycle, deadline, geography, official source, last checked date and status. Respect CC BY-NC 4.0; do not republish the content commercially without separate permission.
