## What the repository actually contains

A large personal PhD application archive and link directory. Its 56-entry tracked tree includes applicant CV, SOP, personal statements, research proposals and sample letters, several recommendation-letter documents, transcripts/test preparation materials, GRE/IELTS content, assorted PDFs, template ZIP sources and a README that links external tutorials on admissions writing, research plans and potential supervisor contact.

The README has substantive curation: it distinguishes a personal statement from a research-oriented statement of purpose, links official campus writing resources and annotated examples, recommends checking each program's actual prompt, and distinguishes thesis research plans from application narratives. This is potentially useful historical editorial context; it is not a funding database, application outcome study or program-specific verified admissions requirement.

## Research and document-transfer lessons

The strongest portable idea is a **genre-specific document workflow**: (1) research contributions and goals belong in the academic SOP; (2) personal/identity narrative belongs only where requested by a program; (3) a research proposal requires hypotheses, methods, and a feasible evaluation plan; (4) actual faculty-fit statements must be independently tied to current publications; (5) recommendation letters must come from authorized referees.

The README's historical GRE requirement spreadsheet is not proof of 2026/27 entrance policies. External SOP and graduate-admissions links are discovery leads, not verified current statements from each institution. Sample letters and test guides may have separate authorship and copyright restrictions.

## Privacy, licensing and scope limitation

This is a personal archive containing recommendation letters, test scores, CVs and academic records, which should not be copied into the public research corpus or used as fictitious applicant evidence. The repository's overall Apache-2.0 metadata does not override rights or confidentiality for externally sourced PDFs, GRE preparation texts, templates, or individually authored reference letters.

No sensitive document bodies, sample letters, score reports or PDFs were opened in this investigation. No compiled research-proposal scientific claim, admission result, fee waiver or funded offer was verified.
