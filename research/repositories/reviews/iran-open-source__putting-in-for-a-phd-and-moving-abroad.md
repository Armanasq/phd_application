## 1. Source purpose and population

Public Farsi/English community guidance on seeking a PhD post outside Iran and relocating to the Netherlands. Source headings emphasize University of Groningen (RUG), including arrival, medical/insurance, housing, municipal registration and degree paperwork. The complete tracked Git tree contains 13 Markdown files; all 13 were source-read at the canonical pinned revision. It does **not** publish funded PhD vacancies, current grant contracts, or admission outcomes for an identified applicant.

## 2. Discovery and application funnel

Find a PhD Position.md lists academicpositions, universitypositions, AcademicTransfer, Study in Europe, general job boards and university faculty job newsletters. It distinguishes Dutch institutional vacancy aggregators from broader portals, but makes no empirical claim about scraper recall, job status or applicant funding eligibility. Candidate action: use sources to locate *official* vacancy records and record employer requisition ID, actual doctoral employment contract or scholarship, closing date/time zone, citizenship and sponsor eligibility, and independent official citation.

## 3. Post-offer document and graduate-school workflow

After being admitted.md describes a sequence: supervisor conveys an oral/email decision; departmental administrator requests CV, Master's diploma/transcript, passport, English proof, proposal and forms; RUG uses a Horafinita graduate administration portal and Train and Supervision Form; later graduate school sends contracts/training-budget guidance. The author explicitly notes scholarship-vs-employee administration may differ. **A verbal faculty indication is not a final signed employment/award contract**; accepted/registered/submitted statuses must have separate supporting evidence. File/department names in this narrative cannot confirm present-day RUG operations.

## 4. Immigration and identity-document evidence

Immigration affairs.md concerns Dutch MVV and residence permit mediation via university Immigration Service Desk, passport/antecedents/hosting forms, consular appointment and legalization. The author describes pandemic-era arrival, specific historical legalization charges, embassy response times and visa issuance turnarounds. None are verified as of 2026 and none should be treated as service-level agreements, legal rules or fee quotes. Actual route and consular eligibility depend on nationality, residence, contract category, IND rules and the official consular post at application date. Do not publish private passport, visa or bank details in research commits.

## 5. Arrival, housing, local administration

After Arrival.md mentions airport→Groningen travel, public transport, municipal registration/BSN, local bank account, SIM card, DigiD, healthcare/GP and bicycle rental. Accomodation.md warns July–September housing demand can be competitive, lists short-stay providers and private rentals, and describes possible income-verification alternatives. These are experience-based planning categories. Exact prices, vendor availability, entitlement to rent/health allowances or required income ratios were **not rechecked**; do not infer legal obligations from personal anecdotes.

## 6. Other chapters and empirical limitations

Flight booking.md mentions historical airline student discounts, age and luggage limits and a discontinued service; such fares and student eligibility are per booking and expire. What to pack.md describes optional personal items rather than legal customs/import advice. Furnishing and Grocery Shopping.md provides shop types and used-goods markets. Dutch lesson.md is a brief intention to translate Dutch terms, not a complete course. More Information Sources.md aggregates university links, news, services and historical tax/transport pages. FAQ.md includes subjective climate, discrimination and cuisine impressions from one narrator, not a representative study.
