## Extracted methodological knowledge

Useful workflow: distinguish course-based master's, research master's and PhD; map faculty and labs when targeting research programmes; schedule transcripts and referees early; separate CV verifiable experiences from SOP motivation and trajectory; seek external peer review, verify official programme and deadline requirements, keep alternate application tracks explicit. The author describes month-by-month milestones and potential document translation steps, but these reflect their cycle/institution/country. Strong opinions about low SOP importance, required three recommendation letters, GRE irrelevance and specific GPA thresholds are anecdotal and may conflict with modern admissions expectations.
