## 4. Appraisal and risk
Positive: explicit authentication and limits for AI endpoints, typed schemas, grounding-source list, safe URL parsing, unit tests. Negative: a list of URLs attached to a professor response does **not** establish that each generated claim is traceable to the exact page sentence; hiring status “unknown” remains model-dependent. User-provided private CV/profile and API key are sent to server AI endpoints, and content is forwarded to Google. Browser localStorage is not encrypted storage; uploaded academic documents should be private. Production rate limit relies on configured Upstash: need operational review when absent. No institution salary or recruitment/visa verification and no published admission outcome data. License unverified prevents direct code import.
## 5. Admissions relevance
Borrow concept of separate professor state, structured AI suggestions, source collection and draft-only correspondence. Always verify PI affiliation, DOI/publications and actively recruiting status on official lab site. A subject line drafted successfully cannot mark email as sent.
