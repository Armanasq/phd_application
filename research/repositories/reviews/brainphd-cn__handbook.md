## 2. Problem, authorship and audience
A group of current/incoming overseas PhD students aims to demystify undergraduates' research preparation, application documents, program models, networking, interviews and examples. Intended applicants often have Chinese undergraduate degrees and plan US neuroscience. The corpus is **survivor-enriched** because its examples explicitly collect successful applications.

## 4. Critical assessment and ethics
**Major ethics failure in historical advice:** interview chapter suggests listing a US address even if the applicant is actually abroad to avoid international interview disadvantage. That would misrepresent an applicant's current location and must be explicitly **rejected**. Never falsify addresses, citizenship, visa status or travel eligibility on applications.
Historical focus: GRE/Skype schedules, US neuroscience onsite tours and reimbursement cannot stand in for 2026 immigration or interview rules. Field bias: US programs with rotation and committee admissions are not the same as ETH salaried lab hiring or EPFL centralized EDIC. Author contribution and case experience are genuine content *as published* but not independently corroborated by applicant records. Self-selected success cases cannot justify conditional success probabilities. Cost/stipend country stereotypes are subjective.
Positive: detailed interview project narrative, scientific scope articulation, differentiating research from personal statements, asking knowledgeable questions to PI and student colleagues. No direct funding finder, scheduler, data model or code to verify.

## 5. Transferable application knowledge
Prepare a reproducible 3-min and 10-min summary of your *actual* SLAM/sensor fusion results: motivating problem, benchmarks, novelty, failure modes, contribution and future experiments. Practice with domain and out-of-domain peers. Research lab questions should address supervision structure, equipment, publication support and salary/tuition explicitly. Official program instructions override all historical anecdote. Existing Chinese applicants' stories are useful for common friction, not evidence of current IRCC/UK/Home fees or offers before Dec31.

## 7. Judgment
**REFERENCE as historical first-person research/interview and writing resource; reject misleading address guidance and all current-policy inference.** Credibility moderate for first-person published processes, low for generalization beyond field/date, unknown for factual independent corroboration. International applicant should use official verified portal and truthful data at every stage.
