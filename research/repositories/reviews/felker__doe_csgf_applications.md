**Disposition:** high-priority ethical exemplar corpus for computational-science fellowships, not a current policy authority.

The repository collects voluntarily submitted examples for the U.S. Department of Energy Computational Science Graduate Fellowship. The README describes applicants' biographical material, research statements, programs of study, recommendation letters and transcripts, and asks contributors to redact addresses, phone numbers, reviewer details and sensitive identifiers. The repository includes historical PDF artifacts with cohort/year filenames. It notes changes to the application format and distinction between current and previous fellowship tracks; these historical descriptions are not verified current DOE CSGF rules.

**Research value:** real examples can teach how to structure research programs, connect interdisciplinary scientific work with computing, and organize a fellowship-specific study plan. The README expressly warns that examples are mostly successful applicants and may not represent unsuccessful cases, creating survival bias. Do not infer selection odds or current form requirements from the published examples.

**Privacy and licensing:** the contribution policy requests volunteer consent and states CC BY 4.0 for contributed applications, but individual letters, transcripts, redaction effectiveness, author intent and embedded PDF metadata were **not audited**. Do not ingest unredacted personal documents or recommender communications into a public dataset. Only reuse material after checking per-file provenance and rights. This pass did not open or republish any PDF body.

**Atlas transfer:** assign `ApplicationExample` type with year/track, author consent, source reference, redaction audit and artifact-completeness state; keep official current fellowship requirements in a separate verified registry. No code/test or live fellowship verification undertaken.
