An Indonesian-language static site/resource hub for the 20 June 2020 webinar *Pursuing International Scholarships for PhD Research*, updated for a 27 September 2021 alumni seminar at Universitas Udayana. The repository includes R Markdown files, rendered site HTML, external source links and webinar artifacts. It is not a programmatic grant database or live doctoral-award monitor.

**Value:** candidate scholarship names, preparation questions and webinar-era resources may seed a research inventory. Their historical teaching value does not establish a current funding opening. Award eligibility, currency, stipend duration, nationality, official deadline and the present cycle require independent verification from the funding agency.

**Limitations:** the last visible update is 2021. Linked pages can expire, program rules can change, and presentations can contain subjective advice. The document is about fellowship strategy, not a verified PhD opportunity record. No linked program pages or binary multimedia files were processed in this review.

**Atlas transfer:** store as `Guide/HistoricalResource`, not an open opportunity; extract discovery leads only after validating official program identity and current award conditions.
