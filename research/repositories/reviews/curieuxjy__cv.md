**Status:** PRELIMINARY_FOCUSED, documentary and template-tree inspection; no compilation.  
**Disposition:** selectively reference CV structure with license and example-data checks.

The repository provides three LaTeX template families: a long-form modular academic/research CV based on Awesome-CV (XeLaTeX), a concise one-column Jake's resume (pdfLaTeX), and a denser two-column Deedy resume (XeLaTeX). It includes template source, bundled fonts, rendered preview PDFs, image assets and a guide to translating research achievements into engineering-impact descriptions.

**Transferable preparation insight:** research admissions and industry screening have different audiences. In PhD applications, a complete publication bibliography, contributions, research projects, teaching and verifiable roles can be useful; industry resumes tend toward selected engineering impact. The guide's examples about robotics and SLAM demonstrate how to express problem/method/result, but illustrative impact numbers and GitHub-star counts are **examples, not independently verified achievements**. Do not copy them as applicant facts.

**Evidence limitations and rights:** the README asserts that one-column LaTeX is more ATS-friendly and describes common parser failures. No controlled ATS benchmark, institutional screening policy or tool compatibility results were supplied. Template families have different upstream license notices in the README; bundled fonts, images and modified class files may have separate rights. There is no globally sufficient permission established by one top-level label.

The reviewed tree contains personal profile images and actual CV examples. This audit does not reproduce private-looking applicant details or redistribute fonts, screenshots or PDFs. No LaTeX compilation, PDF text extraction, license audit or ATS test was run.

**PhD Atlas transfer:** treat the academic CV as a **private evidence-backed application artifact**, not an opportunity. Maintain publication DOIs, authorship, contribution, dates and project metrics with original records; render from the applicant's own data. Use templates only when legal reuse and compilation requirements are confirmed.
