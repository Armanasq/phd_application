**Disposition:** historical template/reference lead only; privacy and consent audit required before reuse.

This 2018 repository collects one applicant's PhD application materials, including a public Markdown personal statement and binary PDFs for transcripts, certificates, test reports and CV documents. The repository's value is documenting the **types of artifacts** an applicant assembled for that historical application, not proving any present-day institutional requirements or a successful admissions outcome.

**Important boundaries:** identity documents, transcripts, language/exam scores, personal statements and recommender materials can expose personal and third-party data. Public Git availability does not establish consent for unrelated redistribution, training, analytics or commercial use. Files may also contain sensitive PDF metadata; this review intentionally did not open or extract their bodies.

**Atlas transfer:** a private applicant-artifact inventory should record document kind, provenance, owner, redaction state, required institution and target application cycle separately from public research notes. Use institutional portals to validate current document requirements. Do not import historical scores or statements as applicant evidence.
