**State:** PRELIMINARY_FOCUSED — three long Chinese-language guidance chapters and license inspected; linked institutions not verified.  
**Disposition:** method reference, not current admissions authority.

This repository provides a 2023-era and later annotated guide for Chinese University of Hong Kong students applying to graduate programs, differentiating discipline, degree, destination and applicant origin. Chapter 1 covers information-source credibility, program identity, CV, transcripts, test scores, recommendation letters, cycles and student–supervisor contact. Chapter 2 focuses on discipline-specific pathways. Chapter 3 discusses research groups, reference preparation, school choice and financial considerations.

**Strong methodological principle:** assess admissions claims at their correct scope and separate correlation from causation. The guide explicitly warns about survivor bias in reported admits, applicant origin, changes in admissions over time and misusing one exceptional applicant's trajectory to infer an effect of GRE/GPA. This transfers to PhD Atlas data-science evaluation: record applicant class, target program, application cycle, admissions outcome and sampling mechanism before comparing attributes.

**Preparation workflow:** assemble a grounded academic CV, institution-specific program list, research interests, transcripts, referee materials and SOP; proactively coordinate referee invitations and distinguish application and reference deadlines. Source text also advocates selective supervisor research rather than unsolicited generic contact. These are historically relevant workflow patterns, not evidence of an institution's current application rules.

**Cautions:** statements about demographic quotas, specific admissions committee pipelines, guaranteed full funding, urban safety, school preference, fee policies or test-waiver rules in the guide are opinion/contextual assertions and cannot be treated as verified facts about current institutions. The handbook mixes PhD and Master's guidance and jurisdiction-specific practices. Some advice about authorship of draft recommendation letters is not universal; users must follow each institution's requirements and referee independence. The repository includes an MIT license file, but rights to third-party linked sources remain independent.

**Atlas transfer:** useful applicant-preparation taxonomy and evidence-quality rubric, no automatic policy imports. Use official university pages for deadline, requirements, citizenship restrictions, funding and faculty recruitment; timestamp every program-specific claim. No live verification or tests performed.
