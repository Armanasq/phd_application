## 4. Critical appraisal
The robotics feed includes [Reading Soft Robotics](https://www.jobs.ac.uk/job/DTP262/phd-studentship-in-soft-robotics-and-embodied-intelligence), which the source listing explicitly restricts to Home/UK/Republic of Ireland fee-qualified applicants. The aggregator gives no comparable international fee restriction, demonstrating why a topical hit can be ineligible. News/current timestamp is no guarantee of employer open status. Some category labels overlap: single listing can be counted under robotics and AI. Claim “generated twice a day” cannot be independently checked from delivered snapshot.
## 5. Applicant relevance
**REFERENCE for rapid lead discovery only**. Reopen each university or official job board URL, check status and visa/fee eligibility; only then elevate a job to VERIFIED_OPEN. Since reading is Home-fee-only, not a default financially viable international target; do not infer applicant status.
## 6. Comparison
Unlike [PhD-Seeker](https://github.com/Aghababaei/PhD-Seeker) this is a distributed Atom feed rather than packaged scraper, with less technical transparency. Unlike [academic-job-digest](https://github.com/BaseLayerAI/academic-job-digest), no published persistent source adapters, tests, or PI enrichment. It may have more current links, but cannot serve as ground truth.
## 7. Judgment
**REFERENCE / MONITOR; DO NOT ADOPT AS FULLY-FUNDED VERIFIED FEED.** Recency of published output is observed; refresh frequency and precision unknown.
## 8. Inspection boundary
Inspected README, root tree, XML content, UI opening, stats metadata. Did not execute generator (not published), enumerate or source-check every Atom entry, check link history or license terms. Evidence confidence: high for static content existence and Reading mismatch, unknown for precision and maintenance guarantees.
