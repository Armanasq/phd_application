## Purpose and contents

A Chinese-language annotated collection of resources for North American PhD applications, organized around school choice, whether to pursue a PhD, recommendation letters, supervisor outreach, statements of purpose, standardized tests/transcripts, CVs, personal websites and additional resources.

The author provides short Chinese explanations of why each linked resource is useful. The collection also includes advice on assessing research fit and treating CSRankings as a way to discover labs rather than as a decision rule.

## Methodological value

The most transferable principles are:
- evaluate research fit and potential supervisors rather than relying on rankings alone;
- understand how recommendation letters are prepared and how to support referees;
- contact prospective advisors selectively and with realistic expectations;
- connect SOP claims to research evidence and a coherent agenda;
- distinguish an academic CV from an industry resume;
- use applicant experiences as context, not as official requirements.

The resource provides a useful applicant-preparation taxonomy that can inform PhD Atlas's private preparation workflow.

## PhD Atlas decision

Use the taxonomy and linked sources as research leads. Separate durable methodological advice from current institution-specific requirements. Record source author, publication date, last checked date, claim type and scope. Current application policies must come from official university sources.
