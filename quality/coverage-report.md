# Coverage and systematic-review limitations

**Review date:** 12 November 2026. An honest **systematic mapping in progress**, not an exhaustive global repository catalogue, not a completed systematic literature review, and not a probability model.

## Actual observed search and screening

The candidate inventory contains **404** deduplicated repository identities including unrelated projects, keyword collisions, duplicate project themes and unreviewed small forks. This came from:
- 12 earlier first-page GitHub repository query groups, at most 35 returned rows per group.
- 8 additional focused/multilingual first-page GitHub searches (English, Chinese, Turkish), with variable raw result counts.
- 6 code-search query families returning short matches, yielding additional relevant leads but high irrelevant-noise rate.
- Select extra source-link discoveries and known repo review candidates. All were merged case-insensitively without upgrading discovery to review.

Current screening split: **327 untriaged; 2 screened included for future deep review; 11 exclusions documented; 64 reviewed**. No systematic second-page+ collection or valid global recall denominator has been demonstrated.

## Audit scope

Of **64 source-pinned individual review records**, **8** have the fully structured eight-section *static* research report, **56** only a bounded preliminary note. Source code, settings, issues/release metadata and methodological failure modes were examined to differing degrees; **no upstream tests were executed in a controlled sandbox** in this research pass. Link to original Markdown note is not equivalent to deep audit or empirical validation.

The 8 deep reports include a scraper with verifiable source-level TLS/eval vulnerabilities, source-first research planner, multi-adapter academic job monitor, current PhD feed, browser/outreach CRM, agentic outreach instructions, supervisor-fit rubric, and a Chinese first-person handbook. Attributions/provenance checked at pinned GitHub commits; neither claim of software security certification nor 2026 admissions outcome is made.

Ten session-specific repository investigation reports were merged into `main` through PR #6 on 12 November. They are additive research artifacts and do not change the canonical 63-row repository register until ownership reconciliation and a separate canonical integration decision are completed.

## Formal research limits

- **Selection bias:** curated Awesome lists and famous PhD guides overrepresent English-language CS and successful US applicants; Chinese and Turkish resource discovery remains incomplete.
- **Source dependence:** GitHub derivative scrapers and multiple Awesome lists can all repeat one original account or employer vacancy; see [five direct relations](../data/repository_relations.csv).
- **Temporal errors:** a 2024/25 advertised vacancy is not a 2026 job, and a program with a Nov/Dec 2026 deadline may issue admission decisions only in 2027.
- **Financial interpretation:** an admission portal is not a funded job; international tuition and stipend gaps matter. Twenty lead records span open channels, conditional awards, partially eligible and verification-needed roles.
- **Evidence:** 26 claim records refer to 21 distinct canonical source URLs, but **independent second-pass semantic verification is NOT PERFORMED**. CI structural validation cannot prove web source meaning.
- **Country scope:** eight country-specific primary-source dossiers exist, but not a national census of laboratories, salary, immigration and vacancy cycles.
- **Experience:** six public anecdotes, unrepresentative and largely anonymous; no causal conclusions or calibrated acceptance odds.
- **Candidate:** one peer-reviewed *Measurement* 2023 publication verified externally; degree certificates, English scores and national eligibility not verified in private formal application documents.

## Coverage stopping criteria not met

A documented saturation result needs (a) expanded pages and topic searches, (b) at least two independent secondary channels and citation-chain exploration, (c) exclusion/dedup accounting per pass, and (d) two consecutive expansion rounds with negligible **new relevant eligible** unique findings, not just few raw links. Until those conditions are met, search stays **IN PROGRESS**.
