# Coverage and systematic-review limitations
2026-11-11. Not an exhaustive review.

**Actual discovery:** Twelve English/Chinese repository-search keyword families sampled up to 35 first-page names per query; union + independently known reviews yielded **392 unique raw candidate identities**. Raw matches include spam, unrelated subject areas and accidental acronym collisions. The numerator “392” is not screened eligible or high quality. Additionally, six GitHub code searches returned up to 15 excerpts each; crosswalk pending. No saturation or recall estimate is defensible.

**Actual audit:** Thirty preliminary focused notes exist in `docs/github/reviews/`; they have not all undergone the 8-section deep-review contract. Deep-review reports are separately staged under `research/repositories/reviews/`. No third-party code has been executed in a controlled environment here. Issue/PR checks limited to selected technical projects.

**Geographical coverage:** Eight baseline country notes already exist, but country-wide opportunity search and complete financial/visa comparison are outstanding. UK, Switzerland, Canada, Australia and selected Norway/Luxembourg/Ireland/NZ primary-source leads are being researched, not proven exhaustive.

**Applicant experience:** public posts are anecdotes, not a sampled representative admissions cohort; interpretation must preserve conflicting accounts.

**Missing source channels:** GitLab, many local-language searches, topic graphs, full pagination, public CV/portfolio detail audit, bibliographic co-citation across original papers, historical snapshot links and non-GitHub university-referenced code. Coverage claims in this repository must refer to inspected bounded material.

**Stop rule:** hold an open discovery backlog until at least two additional independent search channels, follow-up query rounds and deduplicated *eligible new* yield statistics support diminishing returns; a 392 search-result threshold does not satisfy this.
