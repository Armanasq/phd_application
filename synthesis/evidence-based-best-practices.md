# Evidence-supported application practices (scope-limited)

1. **Build evidence of specific scientific fit.** Use recent PI articles and a testable problem formulation, not generic names like “AI”. ETH [RobotX](https://jobs.ethz.ch/job/view/JOPG_ethz_zBtPwWHnM0fumyTFso) asks which groups and what applicant has built; [Monash VL4AI](https://vl4ai.erc.monash.edu/publications/) lists recent SLAM and embodied perception work. Evaluation of fit is an analytical judgment, not confirmation of available capacity.
2. **Match publication gates to actual calls.** ETH [Soft Robotics](https://jobs.ethz.ch/job/view/JOPG_ethz_d3lDgHgDI4ewDFQ1Fb) accepts candidates without papers (though project/report required); [Monash DistRobot](https://vl4ai.erc.monash.edu/positions/distrobot-phd/) requires at least one relevant high-ranked publication. Neither rule is universal.
3. **Separate supervisor contact rules by process.** [EPFL EDIC](https://www.epfl.ch/education/phd/edic-computer-and-communication-sciences/edic-computer-and-communication-sciences/edic-how-to-apply/) explicitly says no supervisor required before central application; ETH RobotX selects groups; Monash VL4AI directs prospective candidates to contact a lab. Generic outreach advice is insufficient.
4. **Funding must be complete and applicant-specific.** [Reading studentship](https://www.jobs.ac.uk/job/DTP262/phd-studentship-in-soft-robotics-and-embodied-intelligence) has home-fee-only eligibility despite a relevant technical title. [UKRI](https://www.ukri.org/apply-for-funding/ukri-funded-studentships/) routes funding via institutions; [Australian RTP](https://www.education.gov.au/research-block-grants/research-training-program/research-training-program-frequently-asked-questions-students) allows international students but universities choose recipients.
5. **Do not mistake deadline for expected offer.** ETH soft recruiters *aim* for feedback within 3 weeks but still require 3 interview stages. UBC December application closes long before 2027 intake. No program examined guarantees a 2026 funded offer.
6. **Verify current employer state independently.** [USN #476839](https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/jobs/476839) has contradictory future deadline and expired flag; leave under verification rather than force a decision.
7. **A working information system needs provenance.** [SihengTao planner](https://github.com/SihengTao/phd-application-planner) demonstrates structured claim/status checks, but online availability and computed scores alone do not prove source semantics. Resolve source content and applicant eligibility explicitly.
8. **Protect confidential application details.** No unapproved email/sending, no private document commits, no copied secrets, and no application status beyond RECEIPT-backed submission.
9. **Firsthand experiences are hypothesis-generating only.** Selected [applicant accounts](../research/applicant-experiences/index.md) show waiting and fee surprises, but do not establish cohort odds, causality or universal advisor-contact strategy.

These are conditional methods, not guaranteed success recipes. Each target requires its own official source and candidate document verification.
