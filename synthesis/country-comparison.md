**Financially sustainable if awarded (not ranked by net income)**: Swiss employment with benefits is an attractive model; other stipend packages need actual tuition offset, taxes/insurance and city-specific cost comparison. Applicant nationality not verified and is *not assumed*.

## Anti-ranking rule
Do not compute synthetic odds. Only after hard gates pass (fit, fee waiver, stipend/salary duration, legal eligibility, live status, documents, submission time) compare funded outcomes and publication/career value.
