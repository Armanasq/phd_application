## Executive assessment
Two parallel routes: central HDR graduate research admission + competitive scholarships, and project-lab funded PhD positions with named PIs. International applicants must verify **both living stipend and tuition-waiver/fee-offset**, plus overseas student health cover. Fully funded advertised research places may have strong scientific fit, but additional publication and academic excellence gates can be strict.

## National funding architecture
[Australian government RTP FAQ](https://www.education.gov.au/research-block-grants/research-training-program/research-training-program-frequently-asked-questions-students): RTP supports domestic and international research doctorates; universities administer decisions; support can include stipend, fees offset and allowances separately; universities can use **at most 10%** of RTP allocations for international scholarships. For full-time doctorates, RTP support 3–4 years; eligibility alone is not a funded award.
