## Executive assessment

## Funding architecture and significant 2026 change
[Research Ireland postgraduate page](https://www.researchireland.ie/funding/government-ireland-postgraduate/) directs away from former individual scholarship model. The [Research Ireland Empower programme](https://www.researchireland.ie/funding/empower/) is **invitation-only to 18 eligible Higher Education Institutions**, not directly to candidates. Institutional application deadline **6 Nov 2026** has passed; intended notifications **Jan 2027**, grants start **Mar 2027**. Thus treating Empower as a currently open individual PhD scholarship is wrong. Potential student positions from awardees must wait for university recruitment/official ads.
