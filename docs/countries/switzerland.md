## Executive assessment
Strongest presently evidenced **2026 application pathway with potentially early recruitment feedback** in robotics: ETH Zurich's salaried Soft Robotics Lab positions. RobotX fellowship offers high research fit but 2027 start by agreement. EPFL EDIC is a centrally selected funded doctoral route with September 2027 intake, not a realistic guaranteed 2026 admission offer.

## Operating model and employment
Swiss institutions require program-specific treatment: ETH robotics vacancies recruit directly into salaried employment; EPFL EDIC uses a central selection committee and financing via fellowship or direct lab hiring. Do not generalize to every Swiss university. ETH RobotX explicitly promises fully funded ETH salary and social benefits; no individual salary number printed on notice. ETH Soft Robotics states employment contract with 18-month initial term and annual renewal; funding commitment duration needs clarification. EPFL states all admitted candidates are financially supported, but EDIC committee selection and lab/fellowship are distinct decisions.

Salary employment, initial 18-month contract then renewals. Source [ETH employer](https://jobs.ethz.ch/job/view/JOPG_ethz_d3lDgHgDI4ewDFQ1Fb).
- **EPFL EDIC:** online **1 December 2026 23:59 CET** for following September. No need to secure supervisor before applying. All admitted students are supported financially; admission conditional on committee selection plus school fellowship or direct hire. English tests expected unless native/main degree language English; GRE optional, three referees, no application fee; application cannot be modified after submit. [EDIC official requirements](https://www.epfl.ch/education/phd/edic-computer-and-communication-sciences/edic-computer-and-communication-sciences/edic-how-to-apply/). A separate EPFL overview retains an outdated “Dec 15” in recommendations despite announcing the change to Dec 1; **the dedicated updated application page governs.**
