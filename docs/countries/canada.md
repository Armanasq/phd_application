## Immigration primary
[IRCC graduate study permits](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/graduate-student/how-to-apply.html) documents PAL/TAL exemption for public-DLI graduate students meeting applicable rules as of 2026. It does not eliminate need for a study permit/LOA, biometrics, proof of funds or case-specific review. Never promise two-week processing as a universal service level.

## Eligibility and financial risks
Each program defines academic entry equivalence, official language tests/exemptions, GPA, proposal or statement, supervisor prior contact, and the number/format of letters. Four-year package may include mandatory teaching duties; Canadian living-cost pressure varies dramatically across Vancouver, Toronto, Waterloo, Montreal etc. Need international tuition breakdown and realistic take-home.
