## Hard gates before submission
1. **Live official portal:** employer/program call accepts new applications; passed or contradictory deadlines require original HR verification. A GitHub/aggregator entry is never sufficient.
2. **Scientific fit:** one or two 2025–26 PI outputs, actual methods/datasets and a plausible original research contribution. Avoid inventing project impact or presenting a preprint as accepted peer-reviewed publication.
3. **Applicant factual credentials:** verified official degrees, transcript/grading scale, language evidence or exemption, top-ranked-venue compliance if explicitly required. No undocumented claims.
4. **Sustainable full funding:** own international tuition obligation, stipend/gross salary, contract duration, fees and benefits. Oxford scholarship consideration is not a confirmed award.
5. **Application timing:** portal deadline local timezone, referee receipt, shortlist interviews, **offer** date and start date separately.
6. **Authorization:** actual user permission to submit/contact followed by a private confirmation receipt. Drafting does not grant permission.

## Formal acceptance rule
A role is not 'ready to apply' merely because the source looks attractive or one paper exists. Record each gate with original official source, access date, required artifact, authorized action, actual receipt and next action. No 2026 offer forecast is supported without an official published selection/offer schedule.
