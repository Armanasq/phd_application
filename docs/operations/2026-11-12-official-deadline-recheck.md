## 1. RobotX Fellowship — evidence-backed dossier requirements

The direct ETH Jobs listing explicitly labels a **fully funded**, 100%-employment doctoral fellowship with ETH doctoral salary and benefits and the deadline 27 November 2026. A master's in engineering, robotics, computer science or related field must be completed by August 2027; practical implementation experience, programming, robotics background and English are relevant selection criteria.

ETH requests **one merged PDF**, ideally under 10 MB, containing in order:

1. Research-motivation cover letter identifying relevant RobotX groups and what has been built.
2. CV with publications/preprints.
3. Bachelor's and Master's transcripts in English with the original institution grading scale.
4. Three referees with email addresses and direct phone numbers.
5. One publication, thesis or project report demonstrating research and writing.

The file name must encode the application date and applicant name. The advertised route is the ETH online portal; email/postal submissions are not accepted. Master-degree equivalence, document readiness, professor choices and personal fit are not yet individually verified.

Primary sources: [ETH job listing](https://jobs.ethz.ch/job/view/JOPG_ethz_zBtPwWHnM0fumyTFso), [RobotX centre application page](https://robotx.ethz.ch/education/open-positions.html).

## 3. EPFL EDIC — high-impact applicant and recommender gates

The EPFL EDIC first-round deadline is **1 December 2026 at 23:59 CET**; a smaller later round closes on 15 April 2027. The official programme page distinguishes central academic selection from receipt of a fellowship or a direct laboratory hire. It describes financial support for all admitted students.

Required materials include:
- Transcripts of every attended university with the institutional grading scale, and diplomas or provisional completion evidence.
- CV in English and a **one- to two-page statement of purpose** identifying fields and relevant EDIC faculty.
- Three recommendation letters; referee notifications are initiated through the online application system.
- Passport or official identity document.
- Unprotected PDFs, maximum **2 MB per document**, with certified translations when original language is outside English, French, German or Italian.
- A recognized English-language score where the official conditional requirement applies; IELTS Academic 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 100 are examples. GRE general is **optional**.

The programme warns that an application cannot be updated after submission and that delayed recommendation letters may miss committee review. Thus proof of application submission must not be equated with receipt of complete references; the latter is a separate evidence and readiness checkpoint.

Primary sources: [EDIC how to apply](https://www.epfl.ch/education/phd/edic-computer-and-communication-sciences/edic-computer-and-communication-sciences/edic-how-to-apply/), [EDIC deadlines](https://www.epfl.ch/education/phd/edic-computer-and-communication-sciences/edic-for-applicants/).
